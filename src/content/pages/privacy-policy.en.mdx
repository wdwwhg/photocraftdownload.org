## About this guide

This website provides independent PhotoCraft download links and usage guides. Reading the guides and following download links does not require an account, payment or uploading your photographs. The editor's own data handling is separate from this website.

## Information used when browsing

A browser request sends technical information such as the requested URL, IP address and browser headers to the website's hosting infrastructure. Hosting and security services may process this information to deliver pages, diagnose errors and protect the site. We do not ask for image files to provide a download link.

The homepage detects your operating system in your browser to highlight a relevant download. This check uses the browser's user-agent information locally; it is not an upload of your files or a scan of your computer.

## Browser preferences and optional services

The site can store a language preference in a cookie and a theme preference in local storage. You can remove these through your browser's site-data settings. Clearing them may reset your preferences.

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand page visits and how visitors use this guide. Google may receive technical browsing information and use cookies or similar storage for this measurement. For details about Google's processing, see the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

If analytics, advertising or support widgets are enabled, their providers may receive technical browsing information and use cookies or similar storage. Their processing depends on the active service and configuration. Your browser's privacy controls can restrict third-party cookies and requests. This page does not treat a preference cookie as permission for unrelated tracking.

## Downloads and external websites

Download buttons lead to the official GitHub release, which receives the download request. ArtCraft, GitHub, Adobe and other linked websites operate under their own privacy policies. Clicking an external link leaves this guide. We do not host or modify PhotoCraft installers.

## Questions and corrections

For website content or privacy questions, use the [website repository](https://github.com/wdwwhg/photocraftdownload.org). Public issue reports are visible to others; do not post personal information, private files or account credentials. For the editor itself, use the [official PhotoCraft project](https://github.com/storytold/photocraft).

## Changes

We update the date above when this page changes. New features that process additional personal information should be accompanied by an updated explanation before they are offered to visitors. See also the [terms of use](/terms-of-service).
